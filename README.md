# 📊 Comparative Financial Performance Analysis of Top 5 Indian IT Companies

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is a **Data Analytics and Business Intelligence project** that analyzes and compares the financial performance of five leading Indian IT companies using historical financial data and an interactive **Microsoft Power BI dashboard**.

The companies analyzed are:

* **TCS (Tata Consultancy Services)**
* **Infosys**
* **HCLTech**
* **Wipro**
* **Tech Mahindra**

The project focuses on analyzing **revenue, net profit, EPS, ROE, ROA, ROCE, Net Profit Margin, borrowings, and Debt-to-Equity Ratio** to understand the financial performance and efficiency of each company.

---

The main objectives of this project are:

* Compare the revenue performance of the five IT companies.

* Compare net profit across companies.
* Analyze **EPS (Earnings Per Share)**.

* Measure asset efficiency using **ROA (Return on Assets)**.
* Analyze capital efficiency using **ROCE (Return on Capital Employed)**.
* Compare **Net Profit Margin**.
* Analyze company borrowings and financial leverage.
* Compare **Debt-to-Equity Ratio**.
* Identify the strongest performers across different financial metrics.
* Build an interactive Power BI dashboard for financial analysis.

## 🧮 Financial Metrics Used

### Revenue

Revenue represents the income generated by a company through its business operations.

It is used to understand the size and growth of a company's business.

---

### Net Profit

Net Profit is the amount remaining after deducting expenses, interest, taxes, and other costs from revenue.

It is an important indicator of overall profitability.

---

### EPS — Earnings Per Share

EPS represents the earnings attributable to each outstanding share of the company.

A higher EPS generally indicates stronger earnings available to shareholders.

---

### ROE — Return on Equity

ROE measures how efficiently a company generates profit using shareholders' equity.

**Formula:**

```text
ROE = Net Profit / Shareholders' Equity
```

A higher ROE generally indicates better utilization of shareholder capital.

---

### ROA — Return on Assets

ROA measures how efficiently a company uses its assets to generate profit.

**Formula:**

```text
ROA = Net Profit / Total Assets
```

A higher ROA generally indicates better asset utilization.

---

### ROCE — Return on Capital Employed

ROCE measures how effectively a company uses the capital available to it to generate operating returns.

It is useful for comparing the efficiency of companies with different capital structures.

---

### Net Profit Margin

Net Profit Margin measures the percentage of revenue that remains as net profit.

**Formula:**

```text
Net Profit Margin = Net Profit / Revenue × 100
```

For example, a 20% margin means the company generates ₹20 of net profit for every ₹100 of revenue.

---

### Debt-to-Equity Ratio

Debt-to-Equity Ratio measures the relationship between a company's debt and shareholders' equity.

**Formula:**

```text
Debt-to-Equity Ratio = Borrowings / Shareholders' Equity
```

A higher ratio generally indicates greater dependence on debt financing.
